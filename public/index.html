History

Google sign-ins

While the Google app is in Testing mode, each sign-in lasts 7 days. Sign in again opens Google in your browser: the same account is already picked (any other is refused, so a wrong click changes nothing); click Continue on "Google hasn't verified this app", and leave every permission ticked. Claude Desktop keeps using the old sign-in until you quit and reopen it.
