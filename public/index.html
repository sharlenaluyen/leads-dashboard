History

Every cell the dashboard has written to your spreadsheets, newest first. Also saved in data/sheet-changes.jsonl. Undo puts the old value back if the cell hasn't changed since. Email columns follow Gmail, so the next sync sets those again.

Google sign-ins

While the Google app is in Testing mode, each sign-in lasts 7 days. Sign in again opens Google in your browser: pick the same account, click Continue on "Google hasn't verified this app", and leave every permission ticked. Claude Desktop keeps using the old sign-in until you quit and reopen it.
